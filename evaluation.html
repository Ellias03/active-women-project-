Our Approach to Success

To ensure the Empowering Sport and Fitness initiative delivers real value, we utilize a **Mixed-Methods Evaluation Framework**. We don't just count heads; we measure behavioral change, psychological well-being, and community cohesion to understand the "why" behind the numbers.

Quantitative (The Data)

Focuses on objective, measurable data points tracked directly through our registration systems.

  • Attendance & retention rates against targets.
  • Demographic intake (ensuring diversity goals are met).
  • Pre- and Post-program validated surveys (e.g., Warwick-Edinburgh Mental Well-being Scale).

Qualitative (The Experience)

Focuses on human experience, connecting back to our theoretical frameworks of autonomy and relatedness.

  • Semi-structured focus groups at mid-point.
  • One-on-one exit interviews with participants and coaches.
  • Observation logs recording social interactions and confidence building.
