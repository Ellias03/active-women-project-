Strategy Overview

This roadmap outlines the 12-month rollout of the Empowering Sport and Fitness initiative. We have adopted a phased approach to ensure community feedback is integrated before full scale-up.

Project Timeline

Phase 1: Preparation (Months 1-2)

Focus: Logistics & Hiring

  • Secure venue hire contracts.
  • Recruit 2 specialized female coaches.
  • Finalize insurance and safety audits.
  • Design and print marketing materials.

Phase 2: Pilot Launch (Months 3-5)

Focus: Testing & Feedback

  • Launch "Soft Opening" with 2 sessions/week.
  • Host an open day for community engagement.
  • Collect initial feedback via surveys (Quantitative) and focus groups (Qualitative).

Phase 3: Full Scale (Months 6-12)

Focus: Expansion & Sustainability

  • Increase to full schedule (5 sessions/week).
  • Introduce competitive (but friendly) league options.
  • Apply for Year 2 funding based on success metrics.

Risk Management Log

Potential Risk Impact Mitigation Strategy
Low Participation HIGH Partner with local schools and GP referrals to drive attendance.
Instructor Injury/Absence MEDIUM Maintain a roster of 2 substitute coaches.
Budget Overrun MEDIUM Monthly financial audits and a 10% contingency fund.
